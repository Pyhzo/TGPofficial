Recruitment · TechGamesProduction

Interested in joining the team?

TechGamesProduction (TGP) is the game development team behind Mecapatch. We work together to plan, build, test, and improve Roblox experiences. This page explains what to prepare when applying to become a staff member or contributor.

Recruitment notice: Application availability and open positions have not been confirmed on this page. Follow TGP's official announcements for information about whether recruitment is open and where applications should be submitted.

Who may apply?

People with useful skills

Applicants may have experience in building, scripting, UI design, 3D modeling, animation, art, testing, or another area that supports game development.

People who work well with others

We value clear communication, respect, reliability, willingness to learn, and the ability to work with other team members.

How to apply

Check recruitment announcements

Look for an official TGP announcement confirming that applications are open and explaining the accepted submission method.

Choose the role that fits your skills

Apply for an area where you can contribute. Only choose roles that are listed as open in the recruitment announcement.

Prepare your application details

Introduce yourself, provide your Roblox username, state the role you want, describe your relevant skills, and include examples of your work when available.

Submit through the official channel

Use only the application link or contact method shared through TGP's official channels. Do not send an application to an unofficial account claiming to represent the team.

Wait for the team's response

The team may review your details and contact you about questions, a discussion, or a skills test. Applying does not guarantee acceptance.

What to include in your application

  • Your preferred name or display name.
  • Your Roblox username.
  • The role or development area you are applying for.
  • A short explanation of your skills and relevant experience.
  • Examples of your work, such as builds, scripts, designs, models, or other projects, if available.
  • Your usual availability and how you prefer to communicate with the team.
  • A short explanation of how you could contribute to TGP.

Keep your account safe

Never provide your Roblox password, email password, verification code, two-step verification code, or recovery code as part of an application. TGP does not need these details to review your skills.

What we expect from team members

Communication

Share progress, ask questions when needed, and let the team know when you cannot complete a task on time.

Responsibility

Be honest about your abilities, respect agreed tasks, and take feedback as an opportunity to improve.

Teamwork

Respect other members, cooperate during development, and give helpful feedback.

Willingness to improve

You do not need to know everything. Be ready to learn, practice, and improve your work.
